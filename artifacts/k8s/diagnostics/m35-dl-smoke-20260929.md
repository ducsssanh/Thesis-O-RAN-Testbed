## Runner change

`scripts/k8s/staged.py experiment` now keeps iperf receiver attached to a foreground `kubectl exec` process and records UE `oaitun_ue1` and UPF N3 link counters before and after traffic. This avoids relying on a shell background process and gives the next run enough evidence to distinguish no packet at the UE tunnel from failure to produce the iperf report. The change has syntax and staged chart contract tests, but has not yet been exercised against real traffic.
